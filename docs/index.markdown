## About Me

I am Dr. Alberto Jaenal Gálvez, a Juan de la Cierva postdoctoral researcher at the Robotics, Perception, and Real-Time Group ([RoPERT](https://i3a.unizar.es/es/grupos-de-investigacion/ropert)) at the University of Zaragoza, Spain. Ph.D. in Appearance-based localization in the University of Málaga, with an intership in Meta (former Facebook).

My interests span from Visual **Place Recognition** and **Visual Localization** to **geometry-aware learning**, especially in application to robotics.
